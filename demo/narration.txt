Merchants don’t know which missing details keep shopping agents from recommending their products. Agent Storefront Kit reveals those gaps and lets merchants preview fixes before changing their website.

Start with a store URL. This is a real public storefront, not a fabricated product feed. The kit extracts products and checks eight signals. Sold-out items are recognized as unavailable. Missing information stays unknown instead of being invented.

For Shopify, connect the live catalog. The kit uses official Shop M C P to find products from this merchant, with current prices and availability. Product links stay exactly as returned. Warnings remain visible, and live products are not saved as reusable history.

Next, review the suggested website fixes. Shipping and returns are prefilled from the website when we find them. Otherwise, the merchant gets an editable suggestion, clearly marked as a draft. Proposed changes stay separate from the original check until the merchant applies them.

Ask for the best birthday gift under fifty dollars from this store. The store name changes with the URL. Preview the answer a customer would get from a shopping agent. The language model evaluates the actual product feed. The server checks its product choice, price, budget, and inventory. If nothing fits, it does not invent a match. Compare the current details with your proposed fixes.

The system also works after the merchant steps away. A protected Vercel schedule searches the live catalog, evaluates the store, and saves a completed-check report. The monitoring screen shows when the work happened, what was checked, and which website fixes to review. Reports contain derived readiness information, not cached Shopify products or images.

Discover real products, review missing details, steer the shopping agent, and monitor useful background work. Agent Storefront Kit runs on Vercel for the Agents and Factories track, with live Shop M C P commerce for the Shopify side quest.
